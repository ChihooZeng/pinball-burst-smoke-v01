本机数据

你的语言、音量、操作选项、统计选择、引导状态和最佳纪录由浏览器保存。你可用浏览器的站点数据设置删除这些记录。

可选玩法统计

你可在首页或设置中开启或关闭统计。关闭后，本页面停发并清理未发送的统计队列。选择不影响游玩。

开启后，运营方使用随机浏览器、访问与对局标识、界面语言、波次、游玩时间、构筑、升级、失败原因及暂停记录来评估引导、难度和留存。每次打开页面生成新的访问标识。统计开启时，随机浏览器标识保存在本机，用于去重。关闭统计会从本机移除该标识。统计库不保存 IP、设备指纹、账号或广告标识。若接入受信任的地区路由，统计只标记中国大陆、其他地区或未知。主机的必要传输与安全日志由运营方管理。

事件保留最多 90 天。若需行使数据查阅、删除等权利，可联系运营方。

当前试玩环境未开启玩法统计。

Marble Overload Privacy Notice

Version V1.0 · November 19, 2026

Data on your device

Your browser stores language, audio, controls, analytics preferences, tutorial state, and best runs. You can clear these through your browser’s site data settings.

Optional gameplay analytics

Turn analytics on or off from the title screen or Settings. Turning it off stops future sends and clears the unsent queue in this page. Your choice does not affect play.

When enabled, the operator uses random browser, visit, and run IDs, game language, wave, play time, build, upgrades, failure reason, and pause events to understand onboarding, difficulty, and retention. Each page load creates a fresh visit ID. A random ID stays in this browser while analytics is enabled to deduplicate visits. Turning analytics off removes it from this browser. The analytics database stores no IP addresses, fingerprints, account identifiers, or advertising IDs. When trusted geographic routing is configured, it records only mainland China, elsewhere, or unknown. The operator manages hosting and security logs separately.

Events are kept for up to 90 days. Contact the operator to request access, erasure, or exercise your other data rights.

Gameplay analytics is unavailable in this preview.
